What pushed the score

    Word influence

    red words pushed towards FAKE · green words pushed towards GENUINE · darker = stronger

    Explanation method: the CNN re-scores the review with each word removed and with each review feature reset to the training average; the change in fake probability is that item's influence. BERT and XLNet may weigh things differently. The comparisons describe tendencies in the training data, not rules: no single feature such as length proves a review is fake or genuine.

    How it works. Each model gives a fake probability. A review is shown as FAKE when the average of the three models is 50% or more, otherwise GENUINE. Percentages are shown for the label given: a FAKE result shows how likely it is fake, a GENUINE result how likely it is genuine. Each bar shows one model's percentage; the black tick marks the 50% line.
